Approach

Enhancing natural beauty — face, body, anti-ageing.

Every treatment starts with proportion, not volume. Dr. Sbera studies the face's existing lines — brow, bridge, jaw — before adding anything to them, which is why his rhinoplasty filler and jaw contouring tend to read as structural rather than added on. Results are built to move and age the way a real face does.
